About

I am an AI research scientist and postdoctoral researcher at the XR AI Lab, Yeshiva University (Katz School of Science & Health, New York). My research focuses on building privacy-preserving, federated machine learning systems for large-scale clinical and behavioral data — designing algorithms that work across heterogeneous multi-site datasets without centralizing sensitive records.

My current projects are FUSE (federated fuzzy consensus clustering for incomplete multi-site longitudinal health data, under review at ACM Trans. Computing for Healthcare) and FedAutoCluster (federated clustering where per-site QLoRA-finetuned LLM agents autonomously select ML pipelines, in preparation). Prior work includes real-time BEV-map V2V cooperative perception for autonomous vehicles and mmWave WBAN beamforming (IEEE Best Paper Award, 2020).

Research Projects

FUSE Under Review
ACM Transactions on Computing for Healthcare

End-to-end federated fuzzy clustering system for incomplete multi-site longitudinal health data. Architecture: lossless FeFCM protocol, fuzzy co-association + NMF permutation-invariant consensus aggregation, GapDB composite automatic (K,m) selection, and visualization-aided validation — all without sharing raw records.

6 datasets validated 3M+ health records 88% structural missingness 31:1 site imbalance ARI: 0.494 → 0.593
PyTorch Federated Learning Fuzzy Clustering NMF Missing Data Health AI
FedAutoCluster In Preparation
Distilling Clustering Expert Judgment into Federated Per-Site LLM Agents

Federated clustering framework where each site runs a QLoRA-finetuned LLM agent (Qwen-7B/72B) that autonomously selects its own clustering pipeline and cluster count K from a 49,920-configuration space. Two-tier orchestrator (method-specific protocol + trust-weighted EMA fallback) handles cross-site heterogeneity.

49,920-config space 1,915 expert trajectories 246 datasets (held out) MIMIC-eICU validation
QLoRA Qwen-7B/72B Federated Learning Agentic AI LLM Fine-tuning
V2V Cooperative Perception Published · IEEE TVT
IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology (IF 6.1, 73 citations)

BEV-map based V2V collaborative perception system for autonomous vehicles that resolves occlusion without sharing raw LiDAR. Tested on KITTI — achieves real-time throughput exceeding the 10 Hz requirement. Bandwidth analysis demonstrates the feasibility of BEV-map sharing over V2V links.

0.0456 s/frame latency ~21.9 Hz throughput BEV 1.1 Mb vs LiDAR 4,000 Mb 73 citations
PyTorch Computer Vision BEV Maps KITTI V2V Communication
FeMIFuzzy Published · IEEE IoT Journal
IEEE Internet of Things Journal (IF 8.2, 14 citations)

Novel federated fuzzy clustering algorithm for decentralized incomplete longitudinal behavioral data. Handles missing data through multiple imputation integrated within the federated loop. Achieves ~93% accuracy vs ~52% for decentralized FCM baseline under 20% missingness.

~93% accuracy N=30k, 8 clients (simulation) N=957, 4 real cohorts 20% missingness
